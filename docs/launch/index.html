THE MISSION

Command is more than firepower.

Balance credits and power. Expand production. Read the terrain. Choose when to defend, when to scout and when to commit your armour. The scenario design centres on three contested relay positions and the survival of your headquarters.

01 / BUILD

Establish your base

Keep power online and invest in the structures that sustain your force.

02 / CONTROL

Secure the relays

Contest the north, centre and south of the Frontier Basin for strategic income.

03 / STRIKE

Break the command

Combine infantry, reconnaissance vehicles and heavy armour to pressure the enemy HQ.
