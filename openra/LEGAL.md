# License and asset boundary checklist

- **OpenRA engine/SDK:** GPL-3.0-or-later; if distributing a binary built from it, follow the applicable source-distribution and notice requirements.
- **EA Red Alert source code:** GPLv3 with EA additional terms; preserve notices and do not imply EA endorsement or use EA trademarks as if authorized.
- **Original Red Alert artwork, audio, maps and other game data:** not automatically covered by the source-code license. Do not bundle them unless their separate redistribution terms permit it.
- **Blue Alert original assets:** record the creator, source, license and modification status for every imported asset.
- **Branding:** “Blue Alert: Modern Front” is a working title for an independent project. Do not present it as an official Command & Conquer or EA product.
